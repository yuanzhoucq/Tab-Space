What's New in Tab Space

Release candidates and published releases of Tab Space for Mac, iPhone, and iPad. Safari remains built in; Chrome, Edge, and Firefox use the companion extensions introduced in 4.0.

4.0 for Mac iPhone & iPad 1.0 4.0 RC 3.16 3.15 3.14 3.13 3.12

Version 4.0 for Mac

August 4, 2026
  • Tab Space for Mac becomes a free download with all core features and up to five saved sessions.
  • Add permanent Plus as a one-time purchase for unlimited sessions and all core features.
  • People who bought the Mac app before 4.0 automatically receive permanent Plus at no charge.
  • Add optional AI organizing to name sessions, recommend tags, and split mixed tabs by topic. Free and Plus include five AI requests per week; Pro removes the limit.
  • Add Pro multi-browser support in beta for Safari, Chrome, Microsoft Edge, and Firefox 121 or later on Mac.
  • Pro includes unlimited AI organizing, multi-browser support, and future Pro features, with a seven-day annual trial.
  • Existing sessions are never deleted or hidden when the Free limit is reached.

Version 1.0 for iPhone & iPad

July 21, 2026
  • Tab Space is now available on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, requiring iOS or iPadOS 16 or later.
  • Save and reopen your named sessions on the go, with tags and search on every device.
  • Sessions sync across your Macs, iPhones, and iPads through your own iCloud — no account, no ads, no data collection.

Version 4.0 RC

July 18, 2026
  • Add companion extensions for Chrome, Microsoft Edge, and Firefox 121 or later.
  • Save selected tabs, the current tab, or all windows while honoring pinned-tab and duplicate preferences.
  • Restore a session in the browser where the dashboard is open.
  • Add an authenticated, loopback-only bridge with one-time pairing codes and Keychain-backed credentials.
  • Add a background Login Item helper that keeps the browser bridge and iCloud change listener active after the main app closes.
  • Move browser pairing into the main app window and show pairing before the extension exposes tab-saving controls.
  • Keep tab closing separate from saving so tabs are never closed before the Mac app confirms persistence.
