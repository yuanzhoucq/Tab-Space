Live workspace or saved context?

Tab Groups for now.
Tab Space for later.

Safari Tab Groups keep current work live. Tab Space lets paused work leave the browser without becoming lost.

Apple documentation and Tab Space 4.1 checked August 15, 2026

They solve different problems

Use Safari Tab Groups for a small set of active contexts such as Work, Personal or a trip you are planning now. They are built into Safari, sync through iCloud and can be shared with other Apple users.

Use Tab Space when a window should be saved and closed, when groups are becoming an archive, or when the work crosses Safari, Chrome, Edge and Firefox.

Best workflow

Keep the current working set in Tab Groups. Move paused projects and finished research into Tab Space.

Use both without building a graveyard

Keep it in Tab Groups

The work is active

You want its tabs to remain a living Safari workspace, appear across Apple devices or be shared for collaboration.

Move it to Tab Space

The context matters more than the open tabs

You want to clear the browser, preserve the session, search it later and keep recoverable snapshots outside Safari.

When a project pauses

Save its window to Tab Space, close the tabs and remove the group. Reopen the whole session—or only one page—when the project becomes active again.

When the answer might be in another browser

Tab Groups organize Safari. Tab Space’s Browser Tab Switcher searches open tabs across supported browsers alongside saved sessions. Option-Tab can return directly to the original browser, window and tab without making you remember where the page lives.
