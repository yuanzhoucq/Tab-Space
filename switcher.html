AIOptional

Private by default

AI helps organize what you save. It never watches what you browse.

AI is off by default. After a clear disclosure and your consent, it can name a saved session, suggest tags, or split mixed tabs by topic. Only page titles and URLs are sent—never page contents—and content logging is off. No developer-operated copy is retained after the request is answered.

Your sessions remain in a native database on your device, sync through your own iCloud, and can be exported as Text, Markdown, HTML, or a complete backup. No Tab Space account is required.
