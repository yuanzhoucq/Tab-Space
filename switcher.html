Browsing has a before and an after

Open and saved belong in the same place.

Browsers are good at showing what is open. Tab Space also remembers what you chose to close. Version 4.1 brings both into a single, keyboard-first view, so yesterday’s research is as reachable as the tab beside this one.

01

Switch, don’t hunt

Search titles and addresses, use the arrow keys to choose, then press Return. The right browser and tab come forward for you.

02

Save without leaving

⌘S saves and closes the selected tab. ⇧⌘S saves and closes every open window. Hold Option when you want to save without closing.

03

Find what you closed

Search every saved session locally on your Mac. Archive search is included on every plan, because your own memory should never sit behind another gate.

AI Optional

AI with a narrow job

It organizes what you keep. It never decides what you browse.

AI is off by default. After a clear disclosure and your consent, it can name a saved session, suggest tags, or split mixed tabs by topic. Only page titles and URLs are sent—never page contents—and content logging is off. No developer-operated copy is retained after the request is answered.

Your sessions remain in the native Mac database, sync through your own iCloud, and can be exported as Text, Markdown, HTML, or a complete backup.
