Why Tab Space?

Save Sessions

One click — or one shortcut — saves every open tab as a named session and clears the window. Return to your work anytime.

iCloud Sync

Sessions sync across your Macs, iPhones, and iPads through your own iCloud — no account needed.

Tags & Search

Tag sessions your way, then find any saved tab in an instant by title, URL, or tag.

Automatic Backups

Hourly, daily, weekly, and monthly backups with smart retention keep your saved tabs safe — restore any of them in one click.

Export & Import

Export sessions to Text, Markdown, HTML, or JSON. Switching from OneTab? Your list imports directly.

Multi-Browser

Use the same sessions in Safari, Chrome, Microsoft Edge, and Firefox. A one-time local pairing keeps companion browsers connected to the Mac app.

A tidy home for every browsing session

Research, trip planning, weekend reading — each lives as its own session. Pin favorites, tag everything, and search across titles, URLs, and tags the moment you need something back.

Backed up. Exported. Never lost.

Automatic backups run on a smart hourly-to-monthly schedule, and any session exports to Text, Markdown, HTML, or JSON. Dark mode included — it follows your system, day and night.

Frequently asked questions

Does Tab Space sync between Mac, iPhone, and iPad?

Yes. Sessions sync across your Macs, iPhones, and iPads through your own iCloud, with no account required. The Tab Space app for iPhone and iPad is available on the App Store.

Where is my data stored?

Sessions live in a native app database on your device, outside the browser, and sync through your own iCloud. There is no account, no ads, and no data collection.

Which browsers does Tab Space support?

Tab Space 4.0 supports Safari, Chrome, Microsoft Edge, and Firefox 121 or later on Mac. Sessions stay in the native Mac app no matter which browser saved them.

Which macOS and iOS versions are supported?

Tab Space requires macOS 11 or later on Mac, and iOS or iPadOS 16 or later on iPhone and iPad.

Can I import my OneTab data into Tab Space?

Yes. Export your list from OneTab and use Tab Space's Import from OneTab feature. Your saved tab groups become Tab Space sessions.

Can I export my saved tabs?

Yes. Sessions can be exported to Text, Markdown, HTML, or JSON at any time, and automatic backups keep hourly, daily, weekly, and monthly copies.
