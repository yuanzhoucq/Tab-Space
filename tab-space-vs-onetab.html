Tab Space vs OneTab

OneTab defined the save-and-close tab workflow on Chrome and Firefox. Tab Space brings that workflow to Safari — as a native Mac app with iCloud sync, tags, search, automatic backups, and a companion app for iPhone and iPad. It even imports your existing OneTab list.

The same core idea

Both tools solve the same problem: too many open tabs. One click collapses your window into a saved list you can restore later, freeing memory and clearing your head. If you love OneTab's workflow, Tab Space will feel immediately familiar.

Where they differ

OneTab Tab Space
Platform Chrome, Firefox, Edge (browser extension only) Safari on macOS — native Mac app + extension, plus an iPhone/iPad app
Where your data lives Inside the browser's local extension storage In a native app database outside the browser, synced with iCloud
Sync across devices No built-in sync iCloud sync across Macs, iPhone, and iPad — no account needed
Organization A single chronological list of tab groups Named sessions with tags, favorites, drag & drop reordering, and merging
Search Browser page search Full search across session titles, URLs, and tags
Backups Manual export; data can be lost if the browser profile resets Automatic backups with smart retention (hourly / daily / weekly / monthly), plus export to Text, Markdown, HTML, JSON
Extras Share list as a web page Safari keyboard shortcuts: close duplicate tabs, close same-domain tabs, copy URL as Markdown, open in another browser…
Migration — Imports OneTab exports directly
Price Free Paid, one-time purchase — no account, no ads, no data collection

Why a native app instead of a pure extension?

  • Your tabs outlive the browser. Sessions are stored in a real app database, not extension storage that disappears with a profile reset.
  • iCloud sync without an account. Nothing to sign up for; your data stays in your own iCloud.
  • Continue on iPhone and iPad. Save a session at your desk, reopen it on the couch.
  • Automatic backups. Every save is protected by a retention schedule, so a mis-click never loses a session.

Switching from OneTab

Export your list from OneTab, then use Import from OneTab in Tab Space — your saved tab groups become Tab Space sessions, ready to tag and search.
